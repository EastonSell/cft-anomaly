# CZX-inspired ML research assessment: checked bound and witness search

**Status, 27 October 2026: a research proposal, not an experiment.** The candidate CZX proof is a useful development example, subject to independent specialist and priority review. The original [Lessa–Cheng–Wang paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.17357) already states the conjectured value and provides candidate saturating bases and related block decompositions. The local argument under review adds a candidate lower-bound chain; do not report the entire physical result as newly established by an ML method.

**Verdict.** A concrete direction distinct from the existing knowledge-gradient allocation plan is to learn which *mathematical certificate and extremizer to propose*, while deterministic verifiers decide validity. Test whether witness-conditioned cut proposal closes a certified quantum entropy bound faster than equally budgeted mathematical search. This is not evidence of a new general architecture or theorem. Symmetry compression, learned separation, SOS and certificate-guided proof synthesis already have close prior art. A valuable contribution would require a controlled advantage on held-out, independently sourced problems, not merely a replay of CZX.

## A testable bounded problem

Start from one already-reduced k-qubit block, rather than assume that arbitrary high-dimensional quantum states admit this reduction. Let G be a simple graph with all degrees even, k≥3, and put `U = X_1...X_k product_(ij in E) CZ_ij` and `S = i^|E| U`. Then `S²=I`; fix a sector `S|psi>=lambda|psi>`, lambda=±1. For each local qubit, `S Z_i S†=-Z_i`, so its Bloch vector has zero z-component. Define `x_i=<X_i>²+<Y_i>²`. Using a state-aligned XY observable and its symmetry conjugate, the anticommuting-observable argument yields `x_i+x_j<=1` for each edge. Always also impose `0<=x_i<=1`. With rational nonnegative weights, the pure-state objective is `F_w(psi)=sum_i w_i f(x_i)`, where `f(x)=h((1+sqrt(x))/2)` and `h(p)=-p ln p-(1-p)ln(1-p)`.

The feasible quantum x-vectors lie inside this graph polytope; no equality of the sets is assumed. **f is concave, and the task minimizes it**, so this is not a standard convex-minimization problem. For bounded k, exact rational vertex enumeration plus rigorously enclosed entropy values gives an outer-relaxation lower bound L. An independently checked normalized sector state gives a physical upper bound U_witness. A verified epsilon result requires outward-rounded certificates proving `U_witness - L <= epsilon`; exact equality requires an exact symbolic comparison, not just close floating-point values. Finite-k results do not establish the original all-chain-length theorem. For a specified mixed sector projector, one saturating pure state is insufficient: independently verify an attaining ensemble or basis that reconstructs the projector.

A public negative control is `x=(1/2,1/2,0)` in the three-party triangle. It satisfies all pairwise outer constraints but is not the marginal spectrum of a pure three-qubit state, by the [one-qubit spectral polygon inequalities](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0209085). It is not a vertex and this fact alone does **not** show an entropy-optimum gap. Conversely, the candidate CZX balanced state has `x=(1/2,1/2,1/2)`; treating all three dressed pairs as one fixed pairwise-anticommuting triple would incorrectly give `sum x_i<=1`. The benchmark must reject that false cut.

## Concrete learning intervention

For an input graph, sector, weights, current certified polytope, its minimizing vertex and best verified state, ask a trained model to propose a pair: (a) a nonzero rational vector `c` from the common finite grammar `{0,1,2}^k`, and (b) an algebraic or circuit-form candidate sector state. Do not let the model supply its own unverified inequality. Define `P_lambda=(I+lambda S)/2`, let `P` project onto the symmetric subspace of two copies of that sector, and set

`H(c) = sum_i c_i (X_i tensor X_i + Y_i tensor Y_i)`.

For every sector state, `c dot x = <psi tensor psi|H(c)|psi tensor psi>`. Therefore any **independently certified** beta satisfying `P (beta I - H(c)) P >= 0` proves the valid cut `c dot x <= beta`. Obtain beta by outward rounding and check positive semidefiniteness with exact rational/algebraic arithmetic; a floating-point eigensolver proposal is not a certificate. The learned proposal may depend on the current relaxed minimizer and best physical witness. Verify the state normalization, sector, marginal spectra and entropy upper bound separately; invalid, timed-out and unresolved proposals earn no certificate. A baseline initial state can be obtained by normalizing `P_lambda |0...0>` when nonzero.

**Falsifiable hypothesis:** under the same total cost and checker, the witness-conditioned model will produce more fully checked epsilon-matching lower/upper pairs on held-out problem clusters than the strongest preset non-learning search. The primary outcome is the per-cluster fraction with a complete certified pair before the cap, not an LLM score, an unsupported proof sketch, or the numerical bound alone. Report the paired difference and uncertainty, verified bound gaps at fixed budgets, solver/verification time, rejected certificates, errors and memory. Predeclare the effect margin and power from separate development data before a confirmatory test. If the deterministic method matches it, if extra model/training cost removes the gain, if the model mainly selects cuts the simple method already finds, or if no valid gap closes, the hypothesis fails.

Controls use the **same** valid-cut grammar, initial polytope, state-template grammar, symbolic verifier, model-visible facts and budget. Compare: (1) deterministic enumeration of c and state templates with a fixed ordering; (2) a deterministic gap/relaxed-vertex heuristic and a seeded random ordering; (3) the same model producing cuts and witnesses without witness-conditioned feedback; and (4) an exhaustive small-k oracle or anytime curve. Charge model proposals, verifier calls, numerical searches, retry/rounding, compute, training and tuning; report raw and amortized costs. Include an independently implemented symmetry-aware moment/SOS baseline where its interface is compatible, with honest scope differences. The oracle gives a small-instance ceiling, not a model baseline at equal runtime if it is allowed an unlimited budget.

## Source overlap and hard boundaries

- [SymDPoly](https://arxiv.org/html/1808.09598) already automates symmetry-adapted noncommutative relaxations; its symmetry search uses a supplied candidate group, so do not claim it solves the CZX local-measurement construction. [Ioannou and Rosset](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.10803) derive symmetry-reduced SOS certificates and exact quantum examples. [Naceur et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17713v2) derive exact rational certificates, including for symmetry/sparsity-adapted systems.
- Learned separation of valid inequalities is established, for example [Kim et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17283). [NSPI](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15445) already uses an LLM, symbolic recovery and Lean to certify polynomial inequalities; [Ax-Prover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12787) includes agent-assisted formalization of a quantum-entropy result. These do not establish this entire CZX entropy/measurement/convex-roof chain, but they rule out broad novelty claims for proposing and checking proofs.
- [Xu, Schwonnek and Winter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00753) study graph constraints for Pauli strings, while [2026 PRX block-matrix relaxations](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/6rh2-s3y2) already analyze quantum correlations for cycle graphs. The latter authors' public code calls Mosek and has no verified top-level software license; verify permission and solver access before using it. The paper's correlation objective is not automatically this entropy objective.
- [NPOsym](https://github.com/marieio/NPOsym) and [QMBCertify](https://github.com/wangjie212/QMBCertify) are concrete operator-certificate sources. Their direct support for the present entropy, local-measurement and witness obligations is not established; any wrapper is an adaptation, not a replication. Formal checking also has to certify the source-to-physics encoding, entropy concavity and any local-measurement inequality, not merely matrix identities.

## Minimum feasibility gate

Use CZX's triangle only as development. Candidate **synthetic** structural holdouts are four nonisomorphic even-degree graphs: C4, C5, two triangles sharing a vertex, and K5, with fixed rational weights. They are variations of one generator, not four independent physics papers, and no solution or benchmark has been prepared. Varying chain length, edge subdivisions or physical Eulerian graphs with only three parties cannot create a new reduced structure: even cut degree forces the three inter-party edge parities to be either all zero or all one. Split graph isomorphs, encodings and weight variants together; test independent source families separately before making a broad claim.

**Do not start a model study until:** (1) a domain expert confirms the block model, proof rules and task target; (2) an offline verifier checks the triangle, its known witness and the false-cut control; (3) the two-copy PSD construction produces at least one independently checked cut that genuinely strengthens a relevant starting bound, with an independently verified witness or a documented remaining gap on a held-out structure; (4) source licenses, exact verifier dependencies and honest full-system baselines are available; and (5) enough non-isomorphic tasks and genuinely distinct source families can be curated without exposing answers. For k=5, the fixed-sector symmetric two-copy space has dimension 136, but exact PSD and entropy checking time remains unmeasured. Set hard CPU/GPU, model-token, reviewer and wall-time caps only after a permitted local engineering preflight; stop if the model, checker, certificates or experts are unavailable. The existing local CZX audit is a qualitative seed, not a dataset or randomized policy trace.

If the deterministic operator solver already certifies these cases within the cap, if two-copy cuts add no useful bound, or if the only apparent generalization is relabeling the triangle, do not claim an ML advance. No model inference, solver experiment, benchmark evaluation, paid API, external service, or new physical theorem was run or claimed for this assessment.
