# CZX-inspired ML research assessment: corrected feasibility review

**Status, 27 October 2026: a research note, not a NeurIPS result or an ML experiment.** No model study was run, and no cut has been demonstrated to improve the starting entropy bound. A local algebraic preflight shows that the previously proposed nonnegative two-copy PSD cut menu is entirely redundant, and an exact CPU check independently confirms a four-cycle case. The candidate CZX proof and the general algebraic argument still require independent specialist and priority review. The original [Lessa–Cheng–Wang paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.17357) already states the conjectured value and provides candidate saturating bases and related block decompositions. Do not report the physical result as newly established by an ML method.

**Verdict: stop the specified two-copy PSD menu.** For every even-degree graph, sector and nonnegative coefficient vector in that menu, its exact optimal spectral bound is `beta(c)=sum_i c_i`, so the resulting cut is implied by `x_i<=1` and cannot improve any starting bound. Moreover, the proposed four-cycle objective is already solved exactly by a deterministic method for every nonnegative weight vector. No useful learned-cut benchmark is specified until a genuinely different relaxation produces an independently certified, relevant nonredundant cut on an instance with a real remaining gap. A prospective learned controller would also need to beat strong deterministic solvers after training and verification costs. Symmetry compression, learned separation, SOS and certificate-guided proof synthesis already have close prior art.

## A testable bounded problem

Start from one already-reduced k-qubit block, rather than assume that arbitrary high-dimensional quantum states admit this reduction. Let G be a simple graph with all degrees even, k≥3, and put `U = X_1...X_k product_(ij in E) CZ_ij` and `S = i^|E| U`. Then `S²=I`; fix a sector `S|psi>=lambda|psi>`, lambda=±1. For each local qubit, `S Z_i S†=-Z_i`, so its Bloch vector has zero z-component. Define `x_i=<X_i>²+<Y_i>²`. Using a state-aligned XY observable and its symmetry conjugate, the anticommuting-observable argument yields `x_i+x_j<=1` for each edge. Always also impose `0<=x_i<=1`. With rational nonnegative weights, the pure-state objective is `F_w(psi)=sum_i w_i f(x_i)`, where `f(x)=h((1+sqrt(x))/2)` and `h(p)=-p ln p-(1-p)ln(1-p)`.

The feasible quantum x-vectors lie inside this graph polytope; no equality of the sets is assumed. **f is concave, and the task minimizes it**, so this is not a standard convex-minimization problem. For bounded k, exact rational vertex enumeration plus rigorously enclosed entropy values gives an outer-relaxation lower bound L. An independently checked normalized sector state gives a physical upper bound U_witness. A verified epsilon result requires outward-rounded certificates proving `U_witness - L <= epsilon`; exact equality requires an exact symbolic comparison, not just close floating-point values. Finite-k results do not establish the original all-chain-length theorem. For a specified mixed sector projector, one saturating pure state is insufficient: independently verify an attaining ensemble or basis that reconstructs the projector.

A public negative control is `x=(1/2,1/2,0)` in the three-party triangle. It satisfies all pairwise outer constraints but is not the marginal spectrum of a pure three-qubit state, by the [one-qubit spectral polygon inequalities](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0209085). It is not a vertex and this fact alone does **not** show an entropy-optimum gap. Conversely, the candidate CZX balanced state has `x=(1/2,1/2,1/2)`; treating all three dressed pairs as one fixed pairwise-anticommuting triple would incorrectly give `sum x_i<=1`. The benchmark must reject that false cut.

## No-go for the originally proposed two-copy menu

The previous proposal used `c` from `{0,1,2}^k`, the sector projector `P_lambda=(I+lambda S)/2`, the symmetric subspace `Pi` of two sector copies, and

`H(c)=sum_i c_i (X_i tensor X_i + Y_i tensor Y_i)`,
`beta(c)=lambda_max(Pi H(c) Pi restricted to ran Pi)`.

The resulting inequality `c dot x<=beta(c)` is valid, but **for every finite simple even-degree graph in this setting, either sector, and every `c_i>=0`, `beta(c)=sum_i c_i`**. Thus it is exactly the weighted sum of the existing box inequalities `x_i<=1`. No ranking, training, enumeration or verification strategy can make this menu produce a useful cut. This conclusion does not cover mixed-sign coefficients or a different relaxation.

The local proof exposes the relaxation loophole. Let `P=P_lambda`, `V=ran(P)` with dimension `d=2^(k-1)`, `A_i=P X_i P`, and `B_i=P Y_i P`, with A and B restricted to V. Since `Z_i P Z_i=I-P` and `Y_i=i X_i Z_i`, we have `A_i²+B_i²=P`. Hence on `V tensor V`,

`2[I-A_i tensor A_i-B_i tensor B_i]=(A_i tensor I-I tensor A_i)²+(B_i tensor I-I tensor B_i)² >= 0`.

For nonnegative c, this gives `beta(c)<=sum_i c_i`. For the reverse inequality, let `J=X^(tensor k) K`, where K is complex conjugation in the computational basis. Even graph degrees imply J commutes with S; it also fixes each `X_i` and `Y_i`, and `J²=I`. In an orthonormal J-real basis `e_1,...,e_d` of V, every A and B is real symmetric. The vector `Omega=d^(-1/2) sum_a e_a tensor e_a` is in the symmetric two-copy space, and for every i its expectation of `A_i tensor A_i+B_i tensor B_i` is `tr(A_i²+B_i²)/d=1`. It attains `sum_i c_i`, proving equality. Its Schmidt rank d is greater than one, so it is not a physical `|psi> tensor |psi>` witness. The symmetric spectral relaxation permits this unphysical optimizer.

This is a locally derived argument with an independent exact finite-instance check, not a new published theorem or an expert-reviewed universal proof. The derivation and finite-instance checks are documented in [docs/c4_audit.md](docs/c4_audit.md); see the [package README](README.md) for commands.

## Exact four-cycle witness and consequences for baselines

For `C4` with edges `01,12,23,30` and `c=(1,1,1,1)`, each sector has dimension 8 and the symmetric two-copy sector has dimension 36. The standard-library CPU checker [checks/czx-ml-feasibility/c4_exact.py](../../checks/czx-ml-feasibility/c4_exact.py) (SHA-256 `007200d042f6723502cbc34eb59ae8f53cd5f05197280485850b82fd6515a384`) constructs exact rational matrices in both sectors. A rational PSD check and exact kernel witness give `beta=4`; `3999/1000` is rejected. The proposed cut `sum_i x_i<=4` is weaker than summing the four existing edge constraints, which gives `sum_i x_i<=2`. The exact maximizing two-copy vector has Schmidt rank 8 and is not a single-copy state tensor square.

An independent physical witness saturates the **edge** bound: in the plus sector use `|+>_0 |+>_2 (|00>+|11>)_(1,3)/sqrt(2)`, with `x=(1,0,1,0)`; applying `Z_0` gives the other sector without changing local spectra. Swapping the two bipartition sides yields `x=(0,1,0,1)`. The checker verifies sector membership and the marginals exactly, including a negative control. For any nonnegative weights, the bipartite edge polytope is integral and these witnesses attain its optimum:

`min_{S psi=lambda psi} sum_i w_i f(x_i) = min(w_0+w_2,w_1+w_3) ln 2`.

For unit weights this equals `2 ln 2`, enclosed by `[1.386294361119890618, 1.386294361119890619]` nats. No valid additional cut can improve the C4 lower-bound optimum, because it must keep the attaining physical witness. C4 therefore supplies an exact deterministic baseline and negative control, not an unsolved held-out task.

**Tested stronger relaxation.** In the same C4 sectors, require the symmetric two-replica density matrix to have positive partial transpose across replicas. Physical tensor-square states satisfy this constraint. An exact rational dual check establishes `3 I-H+|Omega><Omega|>=0` on the symmetric sector; Omega is maximally entangled of Schmidt rank 8, so every PPT state has `<Omega|rho|Omega><=1/8`. Thus `sum_i x_i<=25/8` is a valid PPT-based upper bound. It improves the original spectral bound of 4, but remains strictly weaker than the existing edge bound of 2 and cannot improve this instance's entropy optimum. The value `25/8` is a certified upper bound, not a claim of the optimal PPT relaxation. This does not establish an improvement on any unresolved graph. The reproducible standard-library scripts are in [checks/czx-ml-feasibility/](../../checks/czx-ml-feasibility/); [docs/c4_audit.md](docs/c4_audit.md) gives the derivation and [README.md](README.md) gives commands.

## Conditional research direction, not a ready benchmark

One could investigate a genuinely stronger relaxation, such as a rigorously formulated separability/PPT-constrained moment problem or a higher extension. The C4 PPT certificate above does not improve the starting entropy bound. No usefulness on an unresolved instance, scalable certification, fixed action grammar or nontrivial benchmark is established here. First define the new relaxation and certificate format, independently verify at least one cut that improves a relevant bound on a genuine nonzero-gap instance, and compare a fully charged deterministic solver. Only then consider a learned ranking study. Physical witness checking remains separate from operator-certificate checking; unknown or timed-out proposals never create a cut.

If such a task is constructed, a falsifiable comparison would measure independently certified epsilon-matching lower/upper pairs at a fixed all-in budget on held-out independent problem clusters. Give each policy the same inputs, candidate grammar, verifier and budget; compare against deterministic enumeration, a gap/cost heuristic, direct witness optimization and the strongest applicable symmetry-aware moment/SOS solver. Include the closed-form C4 baseline and exact witnesses whenever C4 is in scope. Count candidate generation, all solver and checker calls, failures, model training and inference; an offline exhaustive reference is not a matched-runtime policy. Predeclare a practically meaningful effect and uncertainty analysis. If the alternative relaxation yields no nonredundant cut, the initial deterministic bound is already attained, or total learned-system cost fails to recover, report the negative result and stop.

## Source overlap and hard boundaries

- [SymDPoly](https://arxiv.org/html/1808.09598) already automates symmetry-adapted noncommutative relaxations; its symmetry search uses a supplied candidate group, so do not claim it solves the CZX local-measurement construction. [Ioannou and Rosset](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.10803) derive symmetry-reduced SOS certificates and exact quantum examples. [Naceur et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17713v2) derive exact rational certificates, including for symmetry/sparsity-adapted systems.
- Learned separation of valid inequalities is established, for example [Kim et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17283). [NSPI](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15445) already uses an LLM, symbolic recovery and Lean to certify polynomial inequalities; [Ax-Prover](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12787) includes agent-assisted formalization of a quantum-entropy result. These do not establish this entire CZX entropy/measurement/convex-roof chain, but they rule out broad novelty claims for proposing and checking proofs.
- [Xu, Schwonnek and Winter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00753) study graph constraints for Pauli strings, while [2026 PRX block-matrix relaxations](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/6rh2-s3y2) already analyze quantum correlations for cycle graphs. The latter authors' public code calls Mosek and has no verified top-level software license; verify permission and solver access before using it. The paper's correlation objective is not automatically this entropy objective.
- [NPOsym](https://github.com/marieio/NPOsym) and [QMBCertify](https://github.com/wangjie212/QMBCertify) are concrete operator-certificate sources. Their direct support for the present entropy, local-measurement and witness obligations is not established; any wrapper is an adaptation, not a replication. Formal checking also has to certify the source-to-physics encoding, entropy concavity and any local-measurement inequality, not merely matrix identities.

## Minimum feasibility gate

Use CZX's triangle only as development. Potential **synthetic** structures include C4, C5, two triangles sharing a vertex, and K5, with fixed rational weights; they are variations of one generator, not four independent physics papers. C4 has the exact deterministic solution above and cannot serve as an unsolved benchmark. A five-vertex bowtie graph with weights `(2,1,1,1,1)` is a candidate unresolved instance: an analytic argument plus exact finite checks indicate that the pairwise relaxation has a unique all-half minimizer which no sector state realizes. Compactness then gives a strictly positive qualitative physical gap. This argument needs specialist review, supplies no gap size or physical optimum, and does not furnish any improving cut. See [docs/bowtie_qualitative_gap.md](docs/bowtie_qualitative_gap.md) and [checks/czx-ml-feasibility/czx_bowtie_exact.py](../../checks/czx-ml-feasibility/czx_bowtie_exact.py). A checked stronger relaxation and a genuine benchmark have not yet been established. Varying chain length, edge subdivisions or physical Eulerian graphs with only three parties cannot create a new reduced structure: even cut degree forces the three inter-party edge parities to be either all zero or all one. Split graph isomorphs, encodings and weight variants together; test independent source families separately before making a broad claim.

**Do not start a model study until:** (1) a domain expert confirms the block model, proof rules and task target; (2) an offline verifier checks the triangle, its known witness and the false-cut control; (3) a **different, explicitly specified** relaxation produces an independently certified nonredundant cut on a genuinely unresolved relevant instance; (4) source licenses, exact verifier dependencies and strong full-system baselines are available; and (5) enough non-isomorphic tasks and genuinely distinct source families can be curated without exposing answers. The original nonnegative symmetric two-copy spectral menu cannot pass gate (3). Set hard resource caps after a permitted local engineering preflight, including development and verification costs. The local CZX audit is a qualitative seed, not a dataset or randomized policy trace.

If the deterministic operator solver already certifies these cases within the cap, if the proposed alternative cuts add no useful bound, or if the only apparent generalization is relabeling the triangle, do not claim an ML advance. The C4 and replica-PPT checkers were run locally using exact CPU arithmetic. No model inference, benchmark evaluation, paid API, external service, or independently established new physical theorem is claimed for this assessment.
